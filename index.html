            Privacy & local data

            Handy stores favorites, recent tools, theme, layout, timer, currency, world-clock, meeting-planner, unit preferences, Unit Converter favorites/recent conversions, Currency favorites/recent conversions, and saved QR templates on this device. Calculator history, QR text, and up to 10 recent QR scans may also remain in browser storage. QR templates and scan history are excluded from settings backups and can be cleared locally.

            QR images and camera frames are processed on this device. Camera access starts only when you choose it and stops when the QR panel closes or the tab is hidden.

            Currency exchange requests the latest USD exchange rates from open.er-api.com. Handy caches app files and saved currency rates for offline use. QR and calculator work does not send its content to a server.

            Local feedback draft

            Saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent automatically.
